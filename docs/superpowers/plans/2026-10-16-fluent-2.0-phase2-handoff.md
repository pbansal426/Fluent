### Task 6: Pre-Flight "What Documents You'll Need" Checklist & Skipped-Fields Action List
- **Why (User Value):**
  1. Immigrant families and seniors often get 10 questions into a clinic or USCIS form before discovering they need their **Health Insurance Card**, **Green Card / Work Permit (USCIS#)**, or **Form W-2**. Telling them *up front* right after the form overview which physical cards/documents will be helpful (while reassuring them they can skip any box they don't have handy) prevents mid-form abandonment.
  2. At the end of the form (`mode === 'done'`), any **skipped** fields should be listed clearly in the Bilingual Verification Summary Card (`#review-card`) with a 1-click **"Answer / Responder"** button so users and staff can see both completed answers and any skipped questions on one screen!
- **Files to Modify:**
  - `extension/lib/form-session.js`:
    - Add `export function getRequiredDocumentsSummary(fields = [], userLang = 'English'): Array<{ id: string, cardTitle: string, whereToLook: string }>` that deduplicates `getDocumentHint(f, userLang)` across all fields in the form.
    - Update `buildReviewItems({ fields, values, filled, skipped, translations, typedPrivateLabel, skippedLabel })` so skipped fields (`skipped?.has(f.id)`) are also included with `skipped: true, shownValue: skippedLabel || 'skipped'`.
  - `extension/lib/agent.js`:
    - In `start()`, if `getRequiredDocumentsSummary(this.fields, this.userLang)` is non-empty, append a friendly 1-sentence heads-up to the spoken overview (e.g., *"It helps to have your Health Insurance Card handy, or we can skip it for now."*).
    - Pass `skipped: this.skipped` into `buildReviewItems` inside `getReviewSummary()`.
  - `extension/panel/review-card.js` & `extension/panel/panel.css`:
    - Style `.review-row.is-skipped` and show `"Answer" / "Responder"` on skipped items in `generateBilingualReviewHtml`.
  - `tests/form-session.test.js`, `tests/agent-milestones.test.js`, `tests/panel-ui.test.js`: Add unit tests covering pre-flight document detection and skipped items in the review card.

### Task 7: Multi-Field Batch Preservation + `-teen` vs `-ty` Acoustic Number Metacognition + 12-Language Phrase Support
- **Why (User Value):**
  1. When a caller or user answers multiple fields at once (*"My name is Bryan Lopez and my birthday is May 12, 1990"*), if `Bryan` needs a spelling check (`Bryan/Brian`), `Agent` must still fill the non-ambiguous fields (`1990-05-12`) in that turn before pausing to ask `"Is that Bryan with a Y or Brian with an I?"`.
  2. Over phone audio or clinic mics, `-teen` vs `-ty` numbers (`13/30`, `14/40`, `15/50`, `16/60`, `17/70`, `18/80`, `19/90`) in street numbers or ages are a top source of speech recognition errors when `alternatives` contain the counterpart.
  3. In `Agent.fromSchema`, automatically call `translatePhrases(this.llm, this.userLang)` in `start()` if custom `phrases` were not supplied and `userLang !== 'English'`, so headless/phone sessions greet and prompt in all 12 languages automatically.
- **Files to Modify:**
  - `extension/lib/metacognitive.js` & `tests/metacognitive.test.js`
  - `extension/lib/agent.js` & `tests/agent-milestones.test.js`
